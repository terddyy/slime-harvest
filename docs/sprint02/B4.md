# B4 - Gayle, the helper (done)

## What changed
- New `Gayle` (`scripts/gayle.gd`, CharacterBody2D) added by `Main._place_gayle()` to `Entities` next to Imogen.
  Layered sprite (Hair_4 ginger, pink shirt, purple pants), reuses `Player.ANIMS` rows and tool overlays.
- Follows Imogen; physics layer 2 / mask 1, so she never blocks Imogen but trees, ponds and the stall stop her.
  Stuck for 2 s or farther than 180 px = teleport beside Imogen.
- Day: every 4 s waters the nearest dry crop within 4 tiles. Night: walks to the nearest slime that is eating a crop
  and hits it for 1 damage per second. Slimes ignore her.
- E next to her (< 26 px, checked after the stall) opens `GayleTalk`: one line, Follow me / Stay here, Bye. Pauses the tree.
  HUD hint "E: talk to Gayle". Lines use names only.
- Stay mode: holds position, still waters nearby crops, only hits slimes within 40 px.

## New files
`scripts/gayle.gd`, `scripts/gayle_brain.gd` (class `GayleBrain`: pure `dry_crop_cell`, `is_eating`, `eating_enemy`,
no autoloads so `run_tests.gd` can test it), `scripts/gayle_talk.gd`.

## Public API
`Gayle.mode` (`Mode.FOLLOW`/`STAY`), `toggle_mode()`, `is_following()`, `teleport_to_player()`, `farm`, `player`,
`walk_bounds`. `GayleTalk.open()/close()/is_open()`, `gayle`.

## Save data
Section `gayle` (registered in `Main._register_save_systems` after `player`): `{x, y, mode}` (mode 0 follow, 1 stay).
Missing section on old saves = she starts beside Imogen in Follow mode.

## Gotchas
- `GayleBrain.EATING_DISTANCE` duplicates `Slime.EAT_RANGE + 1` (Slime can't compile under `--script`).
  `Feedback` has its own copy of the "is eating" check; not merged (out of scope).
- Gayle always attacks the closest slime in reach, never the player; her hit uses `take_hit`, so kills pay gold to the player like Imogen's.
- She doesn't path-find; walls of fences/buildings are handled only by the teleport fallback.
- Tutorial/How to Play: only the E line in How to Play was updated; tutorial tips are P3.
- Credits do not mention Gayle yet (P3).
