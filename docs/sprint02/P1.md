# P1 - Economy and restoration balance (done)

## What changed
- `Restoration.PROJECTS` costs raised from 80/150/200/350/500/650 (1930 total) to
  150/300/450/800/1000/1400 (4100 total). Only the costs changed; ids, order and texts are the same.
- `tests/playthrough.gd` rewritten as a "restore everything" bot: waters the field for free,
  plants/harvests 1 tile per 6 s (`ACTIONS_PER_PASS`, `FARM_EVERY`), plants its best seed, buys
  seeds, chickens/cows, up to 4 apple saplings and sword upgrades, collects eggs/milk/fruit, and
  pays the next unrestored project whenever it can. It prints the day each project was restored and
  `RESULT: farm saved on day N`. Exit 0 = saved, 1 = not saved after `MAX_DAYS` (40).
  `--mode=idle` still plays 7 nights only.
- CLAUDE.md run command now uses `--quit-after 600000`; GDD notes the costs.

## Results
Defend mode, 4 runs at the final costs: farm saved on day 13, 21, 21, 14 (average ~17; high variance
because night losses vary). Typical restore days: well 4-5, farmhouse 6-7, coop 8, barn 9,
windmill 11-17, greenhouse 13-21. Income is roughly 150-400 gold/day once animals and apples run.
Prices of seeds, animals, saplings, crops and upgrades were NOT changed (they were already ordered sensibly).

## Gotchas
- A human is slower and less optimal than the bot in some ways (no sleeping, always defends) and
  faster in others (picks crops, fences crops). Treat the day count as a rough guide; owner play-test decides.
- The bot touches Main internals (`_herd`, `_orchard`) in the test only.
- A full run takes about 20-60 s headless.

## Not verified
Real human pace, Windows export, idle playthrough beyond 7 nights.
