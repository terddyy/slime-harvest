# B1 - Buildings + restoration (done)

## What changed
- Each `Restoration.PROJECTS` entry is now a building in the world, standing on its F4 plot.
  Not restored: price sign (name + cost label), a few rocks/stumps, and a faint blue "ghost" of the building.
  Restored: full sprite with walls (collision); windmill sails animate (4 frames, 6 fps). Restoring plays a sparkle puff.
- Restoration board: a signpost at `WorldLayout.BOARD_CELL` (26,13), west of the farmhouse plot. E next to it
  (within 30 px, `Buildings.is_near_board`) opens `RestorationBoard` (list of projects, price, what it unlocks;
  click / Enter pays with `Game.restore(id)`). Pauses the game, Esc/E closes. HUD hint "E: restoration board".
- `Main` creates `Buildings` (moved in the tree in front of `Entities` so the flat ghosts draw under the y-sorted nodes)
  and `RestorationBoard`; `_interact` checks stall, then board, then harvest.
- How to Play, GDD, ASSETS (building sheets, signs, rubble) and CLAUDE.md Layout updated.

## New files
`scripts/building_data.gd` (BuildingData: SPECS per id, `spec(id)`, `foot_position(id)`), `building.gd` (Building),
`buildings.gd` (Buildings: `get_building(id)`, `is_near_board(pos)`, `board_position`), `restoration_board.gd` (RestorationBoard: `open()`, `close()`).

## For later tickets
- Building `position` = its foot = bottom-middle of the plot (3 px above the plot edge). For the farmhouse this is
  exactly in front of the door: B2 can use `Buildings.get_building("farmhouse").position` as the door spot and
  `building.restored` to check it. Walls cover the whole bottom of the sprite (the door is not walkable); B2 should
  trigger on distance to the foot, like the stall.
- Coop: the first of the 3 coop variants is used; it is taller than its plot (6x5 cells) and overlaps the bottom
  edge of `pen_cows` by ~8 px of roof. B3 pens are unaffected (plots stay blocked).
- Sync: `Buildings` listens to `Game.stats_changed` (loads, no animation) and `project_restored` (animated).
- Plots stay in `farm.blocked`, the board cell is added to `WorldLayout.blocked_cells()` and kept clear of trees/rocks.

## Save data
None added; `Game.restored` (F3) is what the buildings read. Loading a save re-syncs them.

## Gotchas
- Ghost sprites are siblings under `Buildings` (earlier in the tree than `Entities`); they are not y-sorted.
- The unrestored sign label (font 6) sits above the sign and can be cut off at the screen edge only.
- Slimes still ignore buildings (no collision, as with trees).
