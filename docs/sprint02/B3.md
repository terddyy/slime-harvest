# B3 - Animals (done)

## What changed
- Chickens (need the restored `chicken_coop`) and cows (need the restored `barn`) are bought at the stall.
  They wander inside `pen_chickens` / `pen_cows`, give one egg/milk per day, show a bubble, and are
  collected with E (near) or a click on the animal (Imogen within 56 px). Slimes ignore them.
- Products go to `Game.products_in_bag`; "Sell goods" in the shop sells crops and products together.

## New files
- `scripts/animal_types.gd` (`AnimalTypes`): `TYPES`, `PRODUCTS`, `block_reason(id, restored, owned, gold)`. Pure data.
- `scripts/animal.gd` (`Animal`), `scripts/herd.gd` (`Herd`, child of Main, `entities` + `player` set by Main).

## Public API
- Game: `animals` (id -> count), `products_in_bag`, `animal_count(id)`, `add_product(id)`, `buy_animal(id) -> bool`,
  signal `animal_bought(id)`. `bag_value()` and `sell_crops()` include products.
- Herd: `animals()`, `animal_ready_near(pos)`, `collect_near(pos)`, `spawn(type, at, last_collected)`,
  `Herd.pen_rect(type)`, signal `collected(world_pos, product_id)`.

## Save data
- game section: `animals`, `products_in_bag` (old saves without them load as empty).
- new section `herd`: `animals: [{type, x, y, day}]`. Registered after `game` (it needs `Game.animals`):
  from_save uses saved positions up to the `Game.animals` count and spawns any missing animal at random.
- "Product ready" is derived from `Game.day > collected_day`, so a sleep that skips days (B2) just works.

## Gotchas
- Pens are not drawn (no fence art placed); animals simply stay within the pen rect shrunk by 8 px. B1 may draw the pens.
- If a building is not restored, its animals can still exist only through a save made with them; buying is blocked.
- Prices (chicken 40g, cow 120g; egg 6g, milk 18g) are first guesses for P1.
- Tests: `run_tests.gd` `_test_animals` (data, buying rules, selling, save); `aim_test.tscn` `_test_animals`
  (spawn in pen, E collect, save/load of the herd).
