# B2: More crops

What changed
- `Crop.TYPES` has 10 crops (was 4), in shop order by seed price: radish, carrot, potato, tomato, cabbage, corn, eggplant,
  pumpkin, strawberry, chili. New ones use rows 18, 16, 7, 3, 17, 9 of `Crops.png` and the matching Food icons
  (mapped in `docs/ASSETS.md`).
- Strawberry and chili have `requires: "greenhouse"`. `Crop.is_unlocked(id, restored)`; `Game.buy_seed` refuses locked seeds;
  the shop dims the row and says "Needs the greenhouse." (also in the item description).
- Shop: button rows are 17 px high (zero vertical margin) so the longer seed list still fits 480x270.
- Playthrough bot only buys unlocked crops. GDD crop table and ideas list updated.

Testing: unit tests 1161 checks (data order and prices, requirements, locked purchase), aim test, playthrough in defend mode
twice: farm saved on day 13 and day 16 (target 15-20). Shop screenshot read at 960x540 (fits, locked rows dim).

Known limits

- Greenhouse is the last restoration project, so its crops are free-play content, not part of the 15-20 day path.
- Crop sprites were mapped from a contact sheet, not checked in-game one by one.
